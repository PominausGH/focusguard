Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Who We Are

FocusShield is operated by Andrew Dainty, a sole trader based in New South Wales, Australia (ABN 65 366 917 788). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean the operator of FocusShield. For privacy questions or requests, email support@focusshield.app.

Overview

FocusShield requires a free account. Your tasks, focus session history, and streaks are stored on our servers and synced across every device you sign into — this data is not local-only.

Data We Collect

Creating an account requires an email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text). Once signed in, the following is saved to our servers and synced across your devices:

  • Tasks you create
  • Focus session history, streaks, and meeting-cost calculations you save
  • App preferences and settings

For responsiveness, the app also keeps a local cache of this same data in your browser's storage, but your server-side account — not the local cache — is the source of truth, which is why your data reappears when you sign in on a new device.

If you send us feedback through the “Report an issue” form, we receive your message and, only if you choose to give it, your email address so we can reply.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app: storing and syncing your tasks, sessions, and settings
  • To send account emails, such as the welcome email when you register
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure
  • To understand aggregate, anonymous site usage so we can improve FocusShield

We don’t sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it for profiling. Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performing our contract with you (running your account) and our legitimate interests (security, bug fixing, and aggregate analytics).

Cookies & Local Storage

FocusShield does not set tracking cookies. Your sign-in session is handled with a bearer token kept in your browser's local storage, not a cookie.

We use a self-hosted, cookie-free analytics tool (Umami) to understand aggregate usage of the site. It does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information.

Third-Party Services

We use a small number of service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Sentry — error and crash reporting, so we can find and fix bugs
  • Brevo — sending account emails to the address you registered with
  • Cloudflare — network delivery and security for our websites
  • Mixkit — ambient sound files are streamed from their CDN

Some of these providers are located outside Australia (including in the United States and the European Union), so your information may be processed overseas.

Data Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When your account is deleted, your tasks, sessions, and settings are deleted from our servers. Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to them.

Your Rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Stop processing your information, or object to how we process it

Email support@focusshield.app from your account’s email address and we’ll respond within 30 days. You can also export your focus analytics as a CSV from the app’s Settings screen at any time.

EU/UK users (GDPR): the rights above include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

California users (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Australian users: you can contact us about how your information is handled. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Deleting Your Account

To delete your account and all associated data, email support@focusshield.app from the email address on your account. Signing out or clearing your browser’s local storage only removes data from that device — it does not delete your account data from our servers.

Children

FocusShield is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, let you know by email.

Contact
