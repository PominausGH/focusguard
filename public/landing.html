Frequently Asked Questions

Is FocusShield completely free?

Yes — 100% free, forever. No subscription, no premium tier, no ads. FocusShield is also fully open source on GitHub.

Do I need to sign up or create an account?

Yes, a free account is required to use FocusShield. Sign up takes seconds and lets your tasks, streaks, and focus history sync across devices.

Why only 5 tasks per day?

The limit is deliberate. Research in decision psychology shows that constraining choices reduces decision fatigue and increases follow-through. You pick what actually matters — not everything.

What Pomodoro timer presets are available?

Three presets: Classic 25/5, Deep Work 50/10, and Sprint 15/5. Each session links to a task so every focused minute is tracked against real work.

Does FocusShield work on mobile?

Yes. FocusShield is a responsive web app that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers. No app store download required.

How does the meeting cost calculator work?

Enter your team size and average salary. FocusShield calculates and displays the running cost of your meeting in real time, in USD — so teams can decide whether a meeting is worth the time.

Can I export my productivity data?

Yes. The analytics section lets you view completed tasks, focus session history, and productivity trends, with an export option for your records.

How is FocusShield different from Forest or other Pomodoro apps?

FocusShield is free (no credit card required) and combines a hard task limit with a Pomodoro timer in a single web app. Forest requires a paid download. FocusShield runs in any browser — just sign up and start.
